# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 42 verified
stories — 4 lead stories, 12 more in cards, 26 in brief, a strip of the day's five
biggest numbers, and a map of where it all happened.

- **Lead stories:** Huang back, Nvidia +31% · Asia surges on AI relief ·
  EU readies 22nd sanctions · first foreigner in orbit.
- **Also:** Wall Street rebounds, Fed hike now certain, Estonia 'on brink of war',
  Brentford 4-0 Everton, Kilauea erupts, and more from 24 November 2026.
- **Date check:** every story happened on, or was first reported on, 24 November
  2026. Claims are shown as claims.

## The entry

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte — two runs
produce byte-identical files. The image is built by `build.py` (Python 3 stdlib +
PIL) from `stories.json`, rendered with headless Chromium via `shot.js`, and checked
against the frozen layout in the spec: 1024×1024, 42 verified stories, a world map
with numbered pins, and a date-check footer. Every story's primary dated source is
listed in `SOURCES-42.md`.
